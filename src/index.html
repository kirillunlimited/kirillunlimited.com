Hi, my name is Kirill Ivanov. I live in Berlin and build things for the web.

I have always been curious how everything works, so becoming an engineer felt natural. At university I tried game development, 3D modeling and computational linguistics, but it was the web that I fell in love with. My favorite part is building interfaces. It is the part users interact with directly, so their needs are easy to understand: performance, responsiveness, accessibility. If I do my job well, nobody notices, and people just have a slightly better day, which is enough to make me happy with what I do. Behind every interface there are complex systems, architectural decisions and the puzzle of how all the pieces fit together, and that is exactly what makes it fun, and I am still not bored of it.

I would not describe myself as an artist who wants to create something new every day. I am more of a craftsman who works on one thing day after day, trying to bring it as close to perfection as possible.

When I step away from the laptop, there are a few things that keep me busy:

  • I travel, because it is the best source of new impressions and the great way to reset the brain.
  • I go to the gym, and these days it is about health and longevity more than looks or results.
  • I play video games, my guilty pleasure, because sometimes it is nice to step away from reality and solve problems of a different kind.
  • I learn German. Although it is a big source of frustration and makes my brain creak, it is necessary to feel at home here.

Still, the biggest part of my day is my family. My wife and I are raising our son, which is the hardest and the best project I have ever worked on.
